# Corpus evidence

Legend:

- `R` -- confirmed by Russian prose in the source;
- `C` -- confirmed by full-paper composition, but not used as Russian phrase evidence;
- `--` -- absent or not safely observable;
- `stable` -- confirmed in at least two sources;
- `functional-local` -- justified by a function represented principally in one source and not promoted to a universal template.

## Stable structural observations

| Observation | A1 | A2 | A3 | A4 | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1. Abstract begins directly with the object or task | R | R | R | R | stable |
| 2. Abstract proceeds from object and parameters to method and supported results | R | R | R | R | stable |
| 3. Introduction narrows from the field through related work to the paper's question | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 4. Literature is grouped by scientific role when a synthesis is possible | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 5. The purpose is stated after the unresolved or newly isolated question | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 6. Problem statement orders geometry, assumptions, variables, equations, and conditions | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 7. A parameter or symbol is defined immediately before it is used | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 8. Formula transitions are short and sequential | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 9. Boundary or coupling conditions are given a physical or mathematical role | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 10. The characteristic equation is introduced through non-trivial solvability | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 11. A results paragraph is organised around one question or comparison | R | C | R | R | stable |
| 12. Conclusions restate the studied scope, main findings, and actual limits | R | C | R | R | stable |

These twelve observations are the structural core of the skill.

## Stable prose and claim observations

| Observation | A1 | A2 | A3 | A4 | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Neutral impersonal or reflexive voice dominates | R | R in abstract | R | R | stable |
| First-person plural is reserved mainly for derivation or navigation | R | -- | R | R | stable |
| Conditions often precede the result: `При...`, `Для...`, `В случае...` | R | R | R | R | stable |
| An exact technical term is repeated instead of decorated with synonyms | R | R | R | R | stable |
| Figures are discussed through a dependence and a comparison, not a list of coordinates | R | C | R | R | stable |
| A mechanical cause is stated only after supporting analysis is available | R | C | R | R | stable |
| Finite calculations are limited to the considered parameters or grid | R | C | R | R | stable |
| Conclusion verbs reflect evidence strength: `показано` is more common than inflated proof language | R | R | R | R | stable |
