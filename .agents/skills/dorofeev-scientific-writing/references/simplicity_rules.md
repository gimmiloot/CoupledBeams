# Simplicity rules

Clarity means visible logical structure, not shortened technical content.

## Sentence shape

- Prefer about 12--25 words for an ordinary sentence.
- Review every sentence longer than 35 words. Split it if it carries more than one main thought.
- Prefer no more than one subordinate semantic level.
- Avoid consecutive participial phrases and two long parenthetical qualifications.
- Put the main result before a secondary qualification unless the condition must logically come first.
- Keep definitions and indivisible symbol lists intact when division would obscure meaning.

## Paragraph shape

Assign each paragraph one function: definition, derivation step, observation, comparison, interpretation, or conclusion. A short transition paragraph is acceptable when it prevents a logical jump.

## Prefer actions to noun chains

Replace an abstract chain with a verb when meaning is preserved:

- avoid: `анализ характера параметрически обусловленной перестройки спектра`;
- prefer: `анализ изменения спектра при изменении параметров`.

Review sequences containing several nouns formed with suffixes such as `-ние`, `-ция`, `-ость`, or `-ство`. Do not alter an established technical noun merely to satisfy this heuristic.

## Keep syntax direct

Prefer ordinary transitions:

- `Рассматривается...`
- `Для описания ... введём параметр...`
- `Из условия ... следует...`
- `После подстановки получим...`
- `Сопоставление результатов показывает...`
- `Для рассмотренных значений параметров...`

Do not replace these constructions with ornate wording to avoid repetition.

## Avoid two opposite failures

- Do not hide several claims inside one long sentence.
- Do not turn connected reasoning into a sequence of telegraphic fragments.

Keep the connective relation explicit: condition, contrast, cause, consequence, or limitation.
