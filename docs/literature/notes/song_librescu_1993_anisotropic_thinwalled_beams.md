# Song and Librescu 1993: Anisotropic Thin-Walled Closed-Section Beams

- PDF: `docs/literature/pdf/song_librescu_1993_anisotropic_thinwalled_beams.pdf`
- Citation key: `song_librescu_1993_anisotropic_thinwalled_beams`
- Bibliographic form: O. Song and L. Librescu, "Free vibration of anisotropic composite thin-walled beams of closed cross-section contour", Journal of Sound and Vibration 167(1), 129--147 (1993), doi:10.1006/jsvi.1993.1325.
- Role for CoupledBeams: source for coupled free vibration in anisotropic composite closed-section beam theory.

## What Matters For The Project

- The paper formulates the dynamic problem for laminated composite thick- and thin-walled single-cell beams of arbitrary cross-section.
- The model includes anisotropy, heterogeneity, transverse shear flexibility and non-uniform torsion.
- It studies free-vibration behaviour and the contribution of these nonclassical effects for cantilevered beams.
- It provides broader composite-beam context for bending--torsion interaction than a solid rectangular strip model.

## Terminology And Notation

- Important terms: anisotropic composite thin-walled beam, closed cross-section contour, single-cell beam, transverse shear flexibility and non-uniform torsion.
- The local PDF is a protected image-based publisher scan; exact symbols should be checked visually in the relevant equations before reuse.

## Critical Places In The Paper

- p. 129: title-page abstract lists the geometry and all nonclassical effects included in the model.
- The formulation sections on the dynamic model and constitutive coupling for closed-section beams.
- The cantilever free-vibration examples and discussion of anisotropy, shear flexibility and torsional effects.
- The conclusions over the complete pp. 129--147 article.

## Mismatch With The Current Model

- The object is a laminated thick/thin-walled closed-section beam, not the current solid rectangular monoclinic rod.
- Its non-uniform torsion theory and state variables are not interchangeable with the verified Voigt--Lekhnitskii Chapter-2 formulation.
- It contains no rigid two-arm angular-joint determinant.

## Use As Source

Use for the broader claim that anisotropic closed-section composite beams exhibit coupled free vibration and require nonclassical effects. Do not transfer its equations or notation into the current model without a separate derivation.
