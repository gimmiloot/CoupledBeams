# Piovan et al. 2008: Tapered Shear-Flexible Composite Beams

- PDF: `docs/literature/pdf/piovan_2008_tapered_shear_flexible_composite_beams.pdf`
- Citation key: `piovan_2008_tapered_shear_flexible_composite_beams`
- Bibliographic form: M. T. Piovan, C. P. Filipich, and V. H. Cortínez, "Exact solutions for coupled free vibrations of tapered shear-flexible thin-walled composite beams", Journal of Sound and Vibration 316(1--5), 298--316 (2008), doi:10.1016/j.jsv.2008.02.044.
- Role for CoupledBeams: source for exact coupled free-vibration solutions in tapered shear-flexible composite beam models.

## What Matters For The Project

- The paper treats tapered laminated-composite thin-walled beams with open and closed cross-sections.
- Shear flexibility includes bending and warping associated with non-uniform torsion.
- Circumferentially uniform and circumferentially asymmetric stiffness laminations produce different elastic couplings and coupled mode patterns.
- A generalized power-series procedure yields frequencies with arbitrary precision and is compared with two-dimensional FEM results.

## Terminology And Notation

- Important terms: tapered thin-walled composite beam, shear flexibility, non-uniform torsion, CUS/CAS configuration, generalized power series and coupled torsional/flexural/extensional modes.
- The paper's taper parameters, lamination codes and coupled-mode labels are source-specific.

## Critical Places In The Paper

- Sec. 2: model description, equilibrium equations, constitutive relations and boundary conditions.
- Sec. 3: generalized power-series methodology and recurrence construction.
- Sec. 4: validation, taper/slenderness/lamination studies and coupled mode-shape labels.
- Sec. 5: conclusions about taper, elastic coupling and the range covered by the solution method.

## Mismatch With The Current Model

- The beams are tapered thin-walled laminated members; the submitted article uses solid rectangular rods of constant section.
- The power-series formulation is not the current frequency determinant or branch-tracking implementation.
- CUS/CAS laminate couplings must not be identified with the project's monoclinic coefficients without a derivation.

## Use As Source

Use for exact-solution and physical background on coupled shear-flexible composite beams, taper and thin-walled profiles. Do not cite it as the source of the current rigid-joint determinant.
